# Code of conduct

Streamdeckify is a small, friendly project about giving old screens a second life. Keep it that way.

- Be kind. Assume good intent, especially from people who are new to Git, to AI agents or to English.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, sexual content or discrimination of any kind, here or in anything linked from here.
- Don't post other people's private information, and don't share photos with someone else's data on screen.

Maintainers can edit, hide or remove anything that breaks these rules, and block people who keep breaking them.

To report something privately, write to the address in the website footer. We'll keep it confidential.
